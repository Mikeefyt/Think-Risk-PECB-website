What you’ll learn

Course focus.

Keep critical operations moving. Plan for disruption and strengthen organisational resilience with the internationally recognised standard for business continuity management.

  • Business continuity principles and ISO 22301 requirements
  • Business impact analysis, continuity strategies and planning
  • Monitoring, review and continual improvement
  • Main concepts, terminology and management system requirements

Who this course is for

Professionals who want an introduction to the standard and its main management system concepts.
