Study at your pace

Make room for
your learning.

Self-paced learning works best with a realistic study plan. The catalogue’s day counts describe course structure; your own study schedule may take longer.

  • Set regular study sessions around your work and other commitments.
  • Review the course access period and learning format before enrolling.
  • Keep notes on concepts you want to apply in your role.
  • Allow time for revision before booking an examination.
Before your online exam

Prepare your setup early.

Read the instructions for your selected examination, check the required equipment and run the PECB Exams application compatibility check before exam day.

The check includes your camera, microphone and screen sharing. Leave time to resolve any issues.

After your training

Understand the
credential process.

Course completion and certification are separate steps. PECB sets the examination, experience and application requirements for each credential.

1

Demonstrate your knowledge

EXAMINATION

Schedule your online examination through myPECB. Choose an available session and follow the instructions for your course.

2

Apply for the right credential

APPLICATION

After passing, submit an application that meets the requirements for the credential you seek.

3

Keep your credential current

MAINTENANCE

Review PECB’s maintenance rules, including any professional development and renewal requirements.

✓

Keep growing

NEXT STEP

Build on your credential with another level or standard when you’re ready.
