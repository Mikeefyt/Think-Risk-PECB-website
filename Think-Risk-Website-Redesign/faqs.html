Choosing & planning your training

Which course level should I choose?

Foundation introduces the standard and key concepts. Lead Implementer focuses on establishing and managing the system. Lead Auditor focuses on assessing conformity and auditing. Tell us about your role and experience so we can help you choose.

How are the courses delivered?

The courses in this catalogue are self-paced and online. You can study around your commitments. Ask us to confirm the learning format, access period and available support for your chosen course.

Does completing a course make me certified?

Certification follows PECB’s examination and application process. The credential awarded depends on the relevant requirements, which can include work experience and project or audit experience. Completing training alone does not guarantee certification.

Can we arrange training for a team?

Yes. For team training, include your participant numbers, roles and learning goals in your enquiry. Explore team training

What is included in the course price?

Enquire about the fees and package inclusions for your selected course.

Studying & examination

How long can I access the course materials?

Course material access and examination deadlines are separate. Enquire about the access terms for your selected course.

How much time should I allow for self-paced study?

The day counts in the catalogue describe the course structure. Your study time will depend on your experience and schedule. Allow time to work through the material, revisit difficult topics and prepare for the examination. Confirm the access period before enrolling.

Do I have to complete Foundation before a Lead course?

Foundation can be useful when you are new to a standard. Entry requirements for a Lead course depend on the selected training. Review the prerequisites and discuss your existing knowledge with Think Risk.

How do I book an online PECB examination?

Schedule your examination through myPECB and choose an available language, date and session. Follow PECB’s booking instructions for your selected course. Read the booking guidance ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Where can I check the examination format?

Use PECB’s official exam list to check the format, duration, language and other details for your selected standard and level. Confirm the applicable exam arrangements before booking. View the PECB exam list ↗ (opens in a new tab)

How do I prepare my device for an online examination?

Read PECB’s technical guidance and run the compatibility check in the PECB Exams application before exam day. This checks items such as your camera, microphone and screen sharing. Read the compatibility guidance ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Enquiries & next steps

Can I enquire from outside Australia or Malaysia?

Yes. You can enquire from any location. Include your country and time zone so we can discuss the course and practical arrangements with you.

Which currency will I see?

Select AUD or MYR at the top of any page to view catalogue prices in Australian dollars or Malaysian ringgit. These are pricing currencies, not restrictions on your location. Your selection is remembered in this browser. Confirm the current fees and tax treatment with Think Risk.

What should I include in a training enquiry?

Include the standard and level you are considering, your role, country, preferred pricing currency and goals. For team training, add the approximate number of participants and preferred timing. If you are unsure of the level, describe the work you want to support.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We’ll help you explore suitable courses and enrolment options based on your training goals.

What happens when I select Submit Enquiry?

The form prepares an email draft. Select Open email draft, then review and send it through your email app. If an email app is unavailable, copy the prepared text into a new email addressed to pecb@thinkrisk.com.au. The enquiry reaches Think Risk when you send that email.
