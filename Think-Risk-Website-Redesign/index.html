How does self-paced online learning work?

You study the course materials online at your own pace, around your work and other commitments. Listed durations describe the course structure; your study time may vary.

Which level should I start with?

Foundation introduces the standard and its main concepts. Lead Implementer suits people who implement or manage a system; Lead Auditor suits those working in audit or assurance.

Does completing a course make me certified?

Certification follows PECB’s examination and application process. Completing training alone does not guarantee certification.

Can you train a whole team?

Yes. Tell us your team size, roles and locations and we’ll help you plan a pathway for your organisation.

Ready to take your next step?

Tell us what you want to achieve and we’ll help you find the right course or team training pathway.
