Think Risk International - PECB training website (redesign)
Prepared 29 October 2026. Updated 30 October 2026 (technical quick wins: lighter images,
text contrast, search/social tags, course structured data). Design status: draft for review, not yet approved.

OPEN THE SITE
1. Extract this ZIP fully to a folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Keep all the HTML files and the "assets" folder together so styles, images and navigation work.
The Manrope and Instrument Serif fonts load from Google Fonts, so an internet connection is needed for the exact typography.

HOME PAGE BACKGROUND VIDEO
The home header plays a real video clip behind the headline, one per PECB standard
(ISO 22301, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001), cross-fading every 8 seconds under a soft
navy and coral/rose tint. Each clip only downloads when it is about to play.
On phones and data-saver connections the still photo is shown instead (no video download).
A small pause/play icon sits in the bottom-left corner of the header (accessibility).

EDITING
- Prices and course descriptions: edit the "standards" and "levels" lists at the top of assets/site.js.
- Colours: edit the variables at the top of assets/styles.css (--navy, --coral, etc.).
  --coral-deep (#ad4c40) is the small coral text colour. It was darkened from #c9584b to reach
  WCAG AA contrast (5.4:1 on white, 5.0:1 on the grey page background, 4.6:1 on pale coral).
  Keep any replacement at 4.5:1 or higher on white.
- Images: keep new photos as WebP or JPG under 200 KB. The logo is 192x192 and the badge 168x203
  (3-4x their on-screen size, so they stay sharp on high-resolution screens).

SEARCH AND SOCIAL SHARING
The site address is set as https://pecb.thinkrisk.com.au/. If it is hosted anywhere else, find and
replace that address in every .html page, sitemap.xml and robots.txt before going live.
- Every page has a canonical link, Open Graph tags (LinkedIn/Facebook) and Twitter/X card tags,
  using the page's existing title and description and assets/og-image.jpg as the preview image.
- The 9 course pages also include schema.org "Course" structured data (name, description,
  provider, AUD and MYR fees, online self-paced, 2- or 5-day workload). If a fee changes in
  assets/site.js, change the matching "price" in that page's <script type="application/ld+json">.
- After going live: check a page with https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/ and a course page
  with https://search.google.com/test/rich-results, then submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
  Page previews and rich results only work once the site is published at that address.
- The header and footer are repeated in each HTML page; change all 16 pages when editing them.

LEARNING PLAN (THE SITE'S BASKET)
The white "Learning Plan" button in the header, and the coral floating button in the bottom-right
corner of every page, open "Your learning plan", a basket shared by every page. Once items are
added the floating button shows the item count and total, and it pulses when something is added.
On smaller screens the header button shows only the bag icon and count.
Visitors can add any of the 9 courses (+ buttons on course cards, course details, course finder),
an additional PECB course, or team training (a quote item). For each standard it shows a
Foundation > Implementer > Auditor pathway tracker and suggests the next level.
Seats can be adjusted and totals follow the AUD/MYR switch.
"Request enrolment" opens the contact page with the learning plan included in the enquiry email.
No payment is taken online. The plan is stored in the visitor's own browser only.
Products and prices: the "catalogue" list near the top of assets/site.js.
